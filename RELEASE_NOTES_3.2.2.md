# BCS Store 3.2.2 — More sources

Based on MGU Store 0.9.40 by Robert Nisipeanu (GPL-3.0-or-later). App id `bcs_store`; installs over 3.x.

- Three sources are added by the Store when they are missing (Settings > Sources lists them):
  - `waliworld/iDrive8-RideTube`: BeeMTube2, YouTube for iDrive 8 (a fork of RideTube; iDrive 8 only).
  - `cauliflowermossgrape/idrive7-apps`: shift lights, Virtual Dyno, G-Force, M Vitals, Fault Scanner and more (MIT; car-tested).
  - `IterioDev/car-apps`: Seats, Ambient Light, Drive Recorder, Turn View, Car Data and more (iDrive 8 and 7; car-tested).
- Includes 3.2.1 (Luxury Carbon, sharp Performance Red, Theme page) and earlier.
