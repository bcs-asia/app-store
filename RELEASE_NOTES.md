# BCS Store 3.4.15

- **Lists load like a web page.** The Apps and Tools lists show 12 tiles at first. Scroll to the bottom: a turning ring shows below the last row (not in a card, no text), then the next 12 tiles appear, and so on. When every tile is shown, the same scroll loads the next sources (three at a time). The ring row can also be pressed. This replaces the ring tile of 3.4.14.
- **News:** the previous / next arrows and the "1 of 4" count are at the bottom now (right); the text stops above them. On iDrive 8 the text is a little smaller to leave room.
- Includes 3.4.14: faster lists (about 25 times in our tests), and the newer community sources load as you scroll instead of all at once.

Update from the USB stick or over the air: the Store shows Update now (3.4.15). Install it, then restart the head unit.
